Here is a summary of the key points from the interview:

Player Availability & Status

    Inactivity vs. Temporary Absence: Clarified the distinction in the tool—setting a player as "temporarily unavailable" acts as a permanent cancellation across all team rounds, satisfying the user's need to hide inactive or injured players without deleting them.

    Active Status Rules: The user feels the current requirement (e.g., playing at least 4 out of 12 matches over a year) is a bit strict for long-term injuries, suggesting a 2-year window instead.

Team Selection Workflow

    Two-Step Communication: The user non-commitally sends out general team lists first (e.g., on Fridays) before revealing specific match/pair line-ups later in the week to avoid player dissatisfaction when substitutions occur.

    Buffer Players: Extra players are intentionally assigned to higher teams as buffers to account for lingering injuries or last-minute dropouts.

Feature Requests & System Integration

    Simplified Team Export: Requests a clean "team-only" text/email or CSV export option (without full pair positions) to easily paste into communication channels or spreadsheets.

    Reserves Management: Expressed interest in a dedicated feature to manage and prioritize reserves so unassigned, active players feel acknowledged rather than left out.

    BadmintonPlayer Direct Sync: Strong demand for direct integration or smooth export/import with BadmintonPlayer to eliminate manual double-entry errors.

Next Steps

    User Testing: The interviewee agreed to act as a beta tester to review proposed interface ideas before further code development.
