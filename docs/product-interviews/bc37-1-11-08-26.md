Executive Summary

This interview was conducted with a badminton team manager/trainer regarding the team creation and squad selection process for club match rounds ("holdrunder").

The interviewee spends significant administrative time (at least 7 hours per round) dealing with squad assembly, last-minute absences, and player communication across multiple teams (up to 8 teams and ~70+ players).
Key Pain Points & UX Friction
1. Lack of Availability Responses ("Tilkendegivelser")

   The Problem: A vast majority of players do not indicate whether they are available or not before deadlines.

   Impact: For example, out of 27 eligible players on a single team roster, 14 provided no response, 10 accepted, and 3 declined.

   Current Workaround: Team leaders manually text/message players individually on Messenger to confirm availability, creating massive communication overhead.

2. Upstream Absence Cascades (The "Domino Effect")

   The Problem: When a high-tier team (e.g., Team 1 or 2) experiences an injury or cancellation, players from lower teams (e.g., Team 3 or 4) are promoted upwards.

   Impact: Replacing one player at the top disrupts multiple lower-tier lineups, forcing team leaders to redo already finalized team sheets.

3. Inflexible Lineup Creation (No Sandbox / Draft Mode)

   The Problem: Users want to experiment with different lineup scenarios (e.g., "What if Player X is injured?" or "How can we balance Men's/Women's doubles against a tough opponent?") without overwriting active team entries or creating entire temporary player profiles.

   Impact: Lineup creators currently have to track alternative scenarios mentally, on paper, or in separate notes.

4. Poor Overview of Team Evolution & Historical Data

   The Problem: Leaders lack a clear visual way to track player progression over time across seasons or rounds (e.g., seeing how a player transitioned from 3rd Doubles to 1st Doubles).

   Impact: It is difficult to assess team growth or review past ranking points visually without navigating back and forth through historical profile pages.

Designer Takeaways & Research Opportunities
Feature Concept 1: Saved Drafts & Scenario Folders ("Mapper / Gemte Holdtilstillinger")

    User Need: Ability to save multiple draft lineups for a single round without publishing them or creating duplicate profiles.

    Research Focus:

        How might we allow team managers to create "Scenario A" (Ideal Team) and "Scenario B" (Backup plan for injuries) and easily switch between them?

        What UI pattern best supports "Draft vs. Published" states for a round?

Feature Concept 2: Historical Team & Player Progression

    User Need: Visual comparison of lineups across past rounds/seasons to see ranking point changes and position shifts.

    Research Focus:

        How can we surface historical round data (e.g., 2024/2025 vs. 2025/2026) directly within the lineup builder?

        What visualization best highlights player ranking growth and position history?

Feature Concept 3: Availability & Response Tracking (Nudges)

    User Need: Faster insight into who hasn't responded and low-friction ways to prompt them.

    Research Focus:

        How can team leaders identify pending responses at a glance across 70+ players?

        Can we introduce automated reminders or one-click messaging integrations to reduce manual follow-ups?
